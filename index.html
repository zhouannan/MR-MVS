MR-MVS: Monocular Guidance with Multi-view Geometry Optimization for Multi-view Stereo

Annan Zhou1, Li Li1, Jian Yao1
1School of Remote Sensing and Information Engineering, Wuhan University

Abstract

Accurate depth estimation is fundamental to 3D reconstruction, with multi-view stereo (MVS) being one of the most widely adopted paradigms. However, both traditional and learning-based MVS methods struggle in weakly textured or specular regions, where photometric matching becomes unreliable. In contrast, monocular depth models exhibit strong generalization and robust relative depth prediction, but lack metric scale and multi-view geometric consistency.In this work, we propose a hybrid framework that integrates monocular depth priors into a learning-based MVS pipeline and refines depth estimates through multi-view geometric optimization. By jointly leveraging monocular priors, multi-view feature matching, and geometry-aware refinement, our method produces more reliable depth estimates in challenging real-world scenarios. Extensive experiments demonstrate that the proposed approach consistently improves reconstruction accuracy and completeness, particularly in weakly textured and highly reflective regions.
