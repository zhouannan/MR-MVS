MR-MVS: Monocular Guidance with Multi-view Geometry Optimization for Multi-view Stereo

Annan Zhou1, Li Li1, Jian Yao1
1School of Remote Sensing and Information Engineering, Wuhan University

Abstract

We propose MR-MVS, a multi-view stereo framework that combines monocular priors with explicit multi-view geometry optimization. MR-MVS leverages monocular depth cues to provide robust initialization and structural guidance in weak-texture or low-overlap regions, while preserving geometric consistency through iterative multi-view refinement. The proposed strategy improves reconstruction completeness and detail fidelity across challenging indoor and outdoor scenes. Extensive experiments demonstrate competitive performance against strong MVS baselines in both qualitative and quantitative evaluations.
